# Paper Status

## Source status

The ICIP 2027 manuscript source, evidence matrix, reference audit, figures,
and reproducibility notes are complete on the `paper/icip2027` branch.

## Remaining gates

- Authors and affiliations are still unknown and are represented by
  placeholders in the publish wrapper. See `AUTHORS_TODO.md`.
- The final ICIP 2027 author kit and PDF checker must be rechecked when the
  conference releases them.
- A local TeX compiler was not available during the initial audit. The blind
  and publish PDF paths are required deliverables, so compilation must be run
  on a host with the official template dependencies before submission.

## Scientific boundary

The paper stops at the measured VSPW validation result for SegFormer-B0/B1.
Cityscapes recovery and other Round 9 storage work are intentionally excluded
from the evidence and manuscript claims.
